The programme

Medicine that grows back.

Sri Lanka's forests hold an extraordinary pharmacy — trees and herbs that have treated fever, wounds and sleeplessness for two thousand years. Many are becoming hard to find in the wild.

The monastery is setting aside its slopes and open ground to grow them again: a living collection that shades the paths, feeds the birds, supplies the monastery's own ayurvedic needs, and teaches anyone who walks through what these plants are and what they do.

Every plant here is brought by someone. Not bought in bulk, not planted by contractors — carried up the hill by a visitor, a family, a meditation group, and planted by hand.
