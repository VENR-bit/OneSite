A note of thanks

To everyone who gave — thank you.

This place stands because of you. A chair, a shelf, a length of decking, a bag of cement, or simply a quiet wish for its completion — every offering, named and unnamed, has become part of it.

Your generosity is now something one can sit inside: shelves holding Dhamma books, a pantry where tea is offered, a veranda that catches the evening light, and a timber deck open to the forest and the valley beyond.

May the merit of your giving bring you health, ease, and steady progress on the path — and may all who find peace here share in that same blessing.

The Vision

A forest library for mindful learning.

The Rideekanda Library Cafe is a tranquil haven where visitors and monastics can immerse themselves in Dhamma study, engage with Buddhist literature, and enjoy a cup of tea amidst the calm of the surrounding forest.

The design integrates indoor reading spaces with an open veranda and an outdoor timber deck — letting the architecture quietly recede into the canopy around it.

Reading Library
Wall-mounted racks, freestanding shelves, and quiet corners for Dhamma study.
Tea & Refreshments
A small pantry and counter for simple offerings — tea, water, a moment to pause.
Forest Setting
Indoor reading rooms open onto a veranda and timber deck under the forest canopy.
Contemplation Space
Designed for stillness — natural light, gentle silence, room to sit with the breath.
