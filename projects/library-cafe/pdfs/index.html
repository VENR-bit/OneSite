The Vision

A Forest Library Cafe for Mindful Learning

The Rideekanda Forest Monastery Library Cafe is envisioned as a tranquil haven where visitors and monastics can immerse themselves in Dhamma study, engage with Buddhist literature, and enjoy a cup of tea amidst the calming embrace of nature.

The design thoughtfully integrates indoor reading spaces with an open veranda and outdoor timber deck, harmonizing with the surrounding forest landscape.
